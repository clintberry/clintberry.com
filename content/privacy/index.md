*Last updated: October 19, 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how Clint Berry ("I", "me", or "my") collects, uses, and protects information when you use this website (clintberry.com) and any applications, tools, or services I operate that link to this policy (collectively, the "Services").

## Information I Collect

**Information you provide.** If you contact me by email or sign in to one of my applications, I receive the information you choose to share, such as your name and email address.

**Information from Google sign-in.** Some of my applications let you sign in with your Google account. When you do, Google shares basic profile information with the application, which may include your name, email address, and profile picture. If an application requests access to additional Google data (for example, Calendar or Gmail), the consent screen will tell you exactly what is being requested, and the application will only use that data to provide the feature you asked for.

**Usage information.** This website uses Google Analytics, which collects standard usage data such as pages visited, browser type, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

## How I Use Information

I use the information I collect to:

* Provide, operate, and improve the Services
* Authenticate you and keep your account secure
* Respond to your messages and requests
* Understand how the Services are used so I can make them better

## Google API Services User Data Policy

My applications' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

* Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features of the application that requested it.
* Google user data is not sold, and is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide the feature, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
* Google user data is not used for serving advertisements.
* Humans do not read Google user data unless you give explicit permission, it is necessary for security purposes, it is required to comply with the law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

## Sharing of Information

I do not sell your personal information. I share information only with service providers that help operate the Services (such as hosting and analytics providers), when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of myself or others.

## Data Retention and Deletion

I keep personal information only as long as needed to provide the Services or as required by law. You can request that I delete your information, including any Google account data an application has stored, by emailing me at the address below. You can also revoke an application's access to your Google account at any time from your [Google Account permissions page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Security

I use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
